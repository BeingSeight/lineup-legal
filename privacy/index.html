We do not collect or store your raw Aadhaar number. Identity verification via DigiLocker returns only your verified name, date of birth, and state — never the Aadhaar number itself.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account, and verify you are 18 or older.
  • To process deposits, trades, and withdrawals accurately.
  • To meet identity-verification and anti-fraud obligations before releasing withdrawal funds.
  • To send you transactional notifications (order fills, price alerts, account activity).
  • To investigate and prevent fraud, abuse, or violations of our Terms.
  • To respond to support requests and grievances.

We do not use your financial or identity data for advertising, and we do not sell personal data to third parties.

3. Identity verification (KYC)

Deposits do not require KYC. Before you can withdraw funds, we verify your identity via DigiLocker's Aadhaar-based e-KYC flow. You are redirected to DigiLocker's own secure consent screen; we never see or store your Aadhaar number, and DigiLocker only returns your verified name, date of birth, and state back to us.

Live match and score data is pulled from third-party sports data providers (for example CricAPI and football-data.org) to price player tokens — this is one-way; we do not send your personal data to these providers.

We do not sell your personal data. We may disclose information if required by law, court order, or a valid request from a regulator or law-enforcement authority.

5. Data retention

We retain account, KYC, and transaction records for as long as your account is active, and for a minimum period thereafter as required under Indian financial recordkeeping regulations (typically several years from the date of the transaction). You may request deletion of your account; identity and transaction records that we are legally required to retain will be retained only for the legally mandated period, after which they are deleted.

6. Your rights & choices

  • Access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction — ask us to correct inaccurate account information.
  • Deletion — request account closure and deletion of data we are not legally required to retain.
  • Withdraw consent — you may stop using the app at any time; some data may still be retained as described in Section 5.

To exercise any of these rights, contact our Grievance Officer (Section 10).

7. Security

We use encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest for sensitive data, role-restricted access to production systems, and audit logging on financial and administrative actions. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to protect your information using industry-standard practices.

8. Age restriction

LineUp is intended for users aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. Age is verified at sign-up and again during KYC.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be notified in-app or via email before they take effect. The "Effective date" above reflects the latest revision.
