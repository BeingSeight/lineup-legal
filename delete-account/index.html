Delete Your Account

LineUp Technologies Pvt. Ltd.  ·  Effective date: 16 July 2026

You can request deletion of your LineUp account and associated data at any time. This page explains how to request deletion, and exactly what happens to your data afterward.

How to request deletion

  1. Email compliance@lineupapp.in from the email address linked to your account (or include your registered phone number if you signed up with phone/OTP).
  2. Use the subject line "Account Deletion Request".
  3. We confirm your identity and process the request within 7 business days.
  4. You'll receive an email confirming your account has been deleted.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile information (name, email, date of birth)
  • Your phone number and login credentials
  • Your watchlists, notification preferences, and in-app activity history
  • Your active session data

What we retain, and for how long

Indian financial recordkeeping regulations require us to retain certain records even after account deletion:

DataRetained forWhy
KYC identity records (name, DOB, state — from DigiLocker verification)As required by applicable financial regulations from the date of your last transactionRegulatory identity-verification recordkeeping
Transaction history (deposits, withdrawals, trades)As required by applicable financial regulationsFinancial audit and tax recordkeeping

These retained records are kept solely for regulatory compliance, are not used for any other purpose, and are permanently deleted once the mandatory retention period expires.

If you have an outstanding wallet balance or an open position at the time of your request, please withdraw your balance and close open positions first — account deletion does not automatically process a withdrawal.
