Delete Your Account & Data

Effective date: 24 July 2026  ·  LineUp Technologies Pvt. Ltd.

This page explains how you can request deletion of your LineUp account and the personal data associated with it. It applies to the LineUp Android and iOS apps. You can request deletion at any time, free of charge, from anywhere in the world.

Option 1 — Delete from within the app

  1. Open the LineUp app and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile → Settings → Account.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm your choice. Your deletion request is recorded immediately.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

If you cannot access the app, send a deletion request from the email address registered to your LineUp account:

Email a deletion request

Send to compliance@lineupapp.in with the subject “Account deletion request” and include the mobile number registered to your account so we can verify it’s you. We respond to and action verified requests within 30 days.

Before you delete: If your wallet holds a withdrawable balance, please withdraw it first. Once your account is deleted, any remaining balance is handled per our applicable terms and regulatory requirements, and the account cannot be recovered.

What gets deleted

When your deletion request is verified and processed, we delete or irreversibly anonymise the personal data we hold about you, including:

  • Your profile details (name, email, mobile number, date of birth).
  • Your KYC identity data and verification records, subject to the legal retention noted below.
  • Your trading portfolio, watchlists, preferences, and support ticket history.
  • Device identifiers and app usage data linked to your account.

What we must retain (and for how long)

Certain records must be kept for a limited period to comply with Indian law — including financial, tax, anti-money-laundering (AML/KYC), and audit obligations. We retain only the minimum data required, keep it access-restricted, and delete it once the mandated retention period expires. This typically includes:

Data typeReason retainedRetention period
Transaction & wallet recordsTax, accounting and financial-audit lawUp to 8 years
KYC & AML recordsPrevention of Money Laundering Act (PMLA) requirementsUp to 5 years after account closure
Records tied to an active dispute or legal claimTo resolve the dispute / meet legal obligationsUntil the matter is resolved

Retained records are not used for any active service, profiling, or marketing. They are stored solely to meet legal obligations and are deleted when those obligations end.

Timeline

We verify and action valid deletion requests within 30 days. Data held only for operational purposes is removed within this window; data subject to the legal retention above is deleted at the end of its mandated period.
